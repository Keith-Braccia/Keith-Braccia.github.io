GRAZE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

GRAZE ("the app") is a single-player arcade game. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. The developer does not run any servers and does not directly collect, store, sell, or share your personal information. The app does use two third-party services — Apple Game Center and Google AdMob — which are described below.

Information the developer collects

The developer operates no servers, has no user accounts or registration, and runs no personal analytics. The only information the app itself handles is stored locally on your device, as described in the next section. Some data is collected by the third-party services below when you use their features.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of information locally on your device only:

  • The player initials you enter (for the on-device scoreboard).
  • Your best scores and recent results.

This data never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app.

Advertising (optional)

GRAZE shows ads through Google AdMob, and these ads are entirely optional. The app does not display forced or banner ads. The only ad is a rewarded ad that you may choose to watch after a run ends in order to continue playing from where you left off. If you don't want to watch it, you simply tap to restart, and you never see an ad. The game is fully playable without ever watching one.

When an ad is shown, Google (and its ad partners) may collect and process information to deliver and measure that ad, such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device information, and ad interaction data. This processing is handled by Google, not by the developer. You can read how Google uses this information here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • About AdMob and advertising

On first launch (or when an ad is first requested) Apple may show a "Allow Tracking" prompt. If you decline, you can still use the app and watch the optional ad; the ad will simply be non-personalized. You can change this choice at any time in your device Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Apple Game Center, the app submits your scores to Game Center leaderboards so you can compare results with other players. Your Game Center alias and scores are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by the developer. Game Center is optional — the app is fully playable without signing in. You can manage or disable Game Center in your device Settings.

Haptics and sound

The app uses the device's Taptic Engine (vibration) and plays sound effects. These features do not collect any information.

Children's privacy

GRAZE is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app serves ads through Google AdMob, it is intended for a general audience rather than children. Any data handled in connection with ads is processed by Google as described above.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
