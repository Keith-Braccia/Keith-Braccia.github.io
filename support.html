Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Hold anywhere to rise, release to fall. Thread your dot through the gates and skim the glowing edges without touching them — the closer you graze, the more you score. Touching an edge ends the run.

What is the Heat multiplier?

Chaining grazes builds Heat, raising your score multiplier through four tiers. Playing it safe lets Heat bleed away, so keep skimming the edge to keep it climbing.

What's the difference between Daily and Endless?

Daily is one seeded course that's the same for every player and resets each day — compete for the day's top score. Endless is a procedurally generated course that keeps getting harder.

My leaderboard scores aren't showing.

Leaderboards use Apple Game Center. Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in your device Settings and connected to the internet. The app is fully playable without Game Center, but scores won't sync globally.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No in-app purchases, and no forced or banner ads. The only ad is completely optional: when a run ends, you can choose to watch a short rewarded ad to continue from where you left off. If you'd rather not, just tap to instantly restart and you'll never see one — the game is fully playable without ever watching an ad.

Does a continue count on the leaderboard?

Yes. You get one continue per run, and every player gets the same one, so a continued run counts on every leaderboard exactly like any other run. It's completely optional — you can always tap to restart instead and never watch an ad.

How do I turn off ad tracking?

Ads are served by Google AdMob. You can decline ad tracking when the app asks, or change it any time in your device Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. Declining still lets you watch the optional continue ad; it will simply be non-personalized.

Privacy

The developer does not run servers or directly collect personal data. The app uses Apple Game Center and Google AdMob, which handle data as described in our Privacy Policy.
